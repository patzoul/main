Language Detector

Record a few seconds of speech (or drop in an audio file) and this page tells you which language it is — entirely in your browser. It runs OpenAI's Whisper model through Transformers.js and reads the language head directly, so you get ranked probabilities across Whisper's 99 languages. Your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

1 · Audio

— ·
5–15 seconds of clear, continuous speech is plenty. Longer clips are sampled at up to three 30-second windows and the results averaged. Files can be anything your browser can play — .m4a (iPhone Voice Memos), mp3, wav, ogg/opus, flac, or the audio track of a video. Recording needs microphone permission (and an https:// or localhost page). On phones expect a slower first run — the model download and CPU inference both take noticeably longer than on a laptop.
